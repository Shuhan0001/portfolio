Hi, I'm Ashton (Shuhan).

I'm a BSc (Hons) Interactive Media graduate of the University of York, with Upper Second-Class Honours. I work where game systems design meets human-computer interaction. My practice ranges from controlled in-engine experiments on player behaviour to speculative and critical design that makes its argument through an interface rather than an essay. Lately I've been moving toward tangible and embodied interaction, building instruments you hold rather than screens you tap.

02 / Works

SELECTED ARTIFACTS

Completed

2.5D NARRATIVE DEMO · UE5

Revive

A controlled UE5 study on how lighting shapes player behaviour. A 3×3 Latin square with nine participants found that everyone rated the Night setting hardest, yet over half found it the most immersive. Custom Blueprint systems, including S_LightingConfig and a lantern-scan mechanic, kept every response uninstructed.
SPECULATIVE DESIGN · CRITICAL RESEARCH · HTML

Cogni-Stream

A speculative critical-design report built around EnGram+, an invasive brain-computer interface that turns knowledge into a subscription tier. Pricing, downgrade penalties and a visible "Status Halo" turn Van Dijk's digital-divide theory and Suarez-Villa's technocapitalism into something you click through, not just read about.
ANDROID · GAMIFIED BEHAVIORAL INTERVENTION

MiniQuest

A native Android app that turns everyday accelerometer data into an RPG reward loop. It grounds response interruption and environmental enrichment in stereotyped-behaviour research from Rapp and Vollmer, and Gross et al. Built offline-first with MVVM, it uses a custom raw-accelerometer step detector that works without native step-counter hardware.

In Progress

Unity · Management Sim · Collaborative

Supermarket Together

A low-poly management sim that blends RPG and building mechanics. Players design their own store layout and run a restock, price and sell loop, with a fluctuating wholesale-pricing system and a staff-morale AI. I handle game design and interaction design, working alongside a dedicated narrative designer and artist.

In Development
Speculative Design · Tangible Interface · Research Through Design

Threshold

A desktop-scale diagnostic instrument, not a companion. Six life indicators, including memory, identity, relationship, agency, purpose and survival instinct, decay silently across a conversation. The project is framed against Locke's continuity-of-memory account of selfhood and Parfit's degree-based identity theory, and against the real shutdowns of companion apps like Replika and Soulmate.
