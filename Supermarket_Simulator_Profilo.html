LET'S
SUPERMARKET

A low-poly management sim about stocking shelves, pricing goods, and growing one small shop into a full supermarket.

Unreal Engine 5 Management Sim Low-Poly Collaborative Team
View the project on GitHub

"Buy low, price it right, keep the shelves full, and lay out a floor plan customers actually enjoy walking through."

00 / Overview

Let's Supermarket blends RPG-style progression with sim-building mechanics, letting players lay out their own store, stock and price the goods, hire staff, and reinvest the profits into bigger spaces and pricier inventory. The demo aims for a small but fully playable slice of that experience, with a handful of buildable structures, a couple of controllable characters, a first batch of sellable goods, and a short story hook to tie it together.

01 / Core Loop

01
Order stock. Restock inventory while wholesale prices are low.
02
Restock shelves. Carry boxes from the loading dock onto the shop floor.
03
Set prices. Stay within the range customers are willing to pay.
04
Open the store. Customers browse the floor and pick up items.
05
Checkout. Scan each item, take payment, and make change.

02 / Systems

01 Staff AI

Recruited NPCs each take on a single assigned role, and their efficiency varies from character to character. Mistreating them lowers morale, and productivity along with it.

02 Dynamic Pricing

Wholesale prices fluctuate like a real market, rewarding players who buy in when costs dip. Each customer also carries their own acceptable price range, and demand falls off sharply once you go beyond it.

03 Store Building

Modular zones like the bakery and produce section can be paired for bonuses, or placed badly enough to hurt sales, like setting a restroom right next to the deli counter.

04 Story Hooks

Hitting a revenue milestone can trigger a conversation with a randomly chosen NPC, who offers a quest that takes real effort to finish but pays off well.

03 / My Role

I handle game design and interaction design on a four-person team, working alongside a narrative designer and an artist who covers both character and environment work.
